FormIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

FormIQ ("the app") is a fitness guidance app developed by YavuzTech that detects exercise form and counts repetitions using your device camera. This policy explains what data the app processes and what it does not.

Camera and Pose Detection

Camera frames are analyzed entirely on your device to detect body pose, count repetitions, and provide form feedback. Camera frames, pose landmarks, and joint angles are never uploaded, stored remotely, or shared with any third party.

Photos

A photo is saved only when you explicitly tap the capture button during a workout. It is stored in your device's own photo gallery (Android: DCIM/FormIQ, iOS: your Photos library). FormIQ does not upload captured photos anywhere.

Analytics

When Firebase Analytics is enabled, FormIQ records anonymous, aggregate usage events, including:

  • Screen views and screen duration
  • Workout started/ended events, including exercise type, repetition count, and workout duration
  • Form-feedback category (e.g. whether guidance or an error was shown), without the feedback text itself

Analytics events never include camera frames, pose landmarks, joint angles, or any text you write in the feedback form. Firebase Analytics may also collect standard device and app diagnostic information as described in Google's Firebase privacy documentation.

App Feedback

If you use the in-app "Send feedback" form, the message you write is sent as an email, opened in your own email app, to an address configured by the developer. FormIQ does not transmit this message anywhere except through the email you choose to send.

Remote Configuration

FormIQ uses Firebase Remote Config solely to retrieve the feedback recipient email address. No personal data is sent as part of this configuration fetch.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers or ad tracking
  • No sale or sharing of personal data to third parties

Children's Privacy

FormIQ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your Choices

You can deny or revoke camera and photo library permissions at any time from your device settings; doing so limits the app's core functionality. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact YavuzTech at privacy@yavuztech.com.
